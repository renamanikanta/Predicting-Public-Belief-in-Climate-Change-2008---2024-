Predicting Public Belief in Climate Change (2008 - 2024)
Despite strong scientific agreement on climate change, public belief in the U.S. remains polarized.
Understanding why people believe—or doubt—climate change is critical for effective policy design, climate communication, and public engagement strategies.

This project answers:

What factors most strongly predict belief in climate change?

How do ideology and perceived scientific consensus compare in influence?

Do age and education still matter once attitudes are considered?
